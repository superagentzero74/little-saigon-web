06 — Phase Roadmap & Supply Control

From launch to scale

The Đồng economy is designed in two phases. Supply is controlled progressively as the user base matures.

Phase 1 · Now
Generous — Launch
High earn rates. Low redemption costs. Goal: acquire users and build daily habit. Daily cap 500 ₫. Demand-minted supply.
Phase 2 · Growth
Balanced — Scale
Earn rates drop ~20%. Redemption costs rise ~20%. Daily cap tightens to 350 ₫. Tier multipliers drive retention. Supply slows.
Key principle: reduce the daily earn cap, not individual activity rates. Users psychologically anchor to per-action values. A lower cap feels like a platform-level decision; a lower per-review rate feels like a pay cut. Protect the per-action rates as long as possible.
