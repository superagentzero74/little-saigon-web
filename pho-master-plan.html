Phở Master

A mini-game marketing tactic for Little Saigon Official. Built to drive daily app opens, partner restaurant revenue, and community engagement.

Mobile · iOS First Embedded in Little Saigon App 6-Week MVP
01 · Overview

The one-line pitch

A 2-minute tap-to-cook arcade game embedded inside Little Saigon Official — where every round ends with a nudge to a real restaurant down the street.

The Thesis
The game isn't the product. The daily habit is. Every bowl served is a cue to visit Little Saigon — the app, and the neighborhood.

Who it's for

Three overlapping audiences: (1) Vietnamese-Americans in Orange County who grew up eating at these restaurants and feel the nostalgia instantly, (2) younger second-gen users who want a casual reason to open the Little Saigon app, and (3) non-Vietnamese food lovers who'll learn dish names and discover where to eat them locally.

Why now

Little Saigon Official already has 420+ business listings, a working Đồng loyalty currency, and an established iOS app. A mini-game slots into the existing ecosystem with no new infrastructure — it uses the same Firestore backend, the same auth, the same rewards wallet. The marginal cost is just the game layer itself.

02 · Strategic Rationale

Why a mini-game beats other marketing tactics

Traditional marketing (ads, emails, push blasts) has diminishing returns. A daily game creates a reason users choose to open the app — which is worth more than 100 push notifications.

⏰
Daily-return mechanic
Daily Rush resets at midnight. Leaderboards reset weekly. Players have a concrete reason to come back tomorrow — the same loop Wordle, Duolingo, and Pokémon GO use.
🎯
Attributable restaurant traffic
Weekly challenges tie scores to specific partner restaurants. When a player redeems a coupon, we know the game drove the visit — measurable ROI for sponsors.
📱
Built-in virality
Share cards after every Rush run. Wordle-style grid screenshots that friends will post to stories and group chats. Zero acquisition cost for each share.
🌱
Cultural-education entry point
Non-Vietnamese players learn "bún bò Huế" and "bánh xèo" through gameplay. Each dish they unlock becomes a reason to try the real thing at a partner restaurant.
💰
New revenue channel
Weekly featured-restaurant slots are a paid sponsorship inventory that didn't exist before. Directory-ad revenue that doesn't require user-facing ad placements.
🔁
Flywheel with existing app
Đồng earned in-game are the same Đồng used across the Little Saigon ecosystem. Game engagement directly pumps the loyalty currency — no separate economy to maintain.
03 · The Loop

How engagement compounds

Every step in the loop drives a business outcome. The game's purpose is to keep users cycling through it daily.

Step 01
Daily push
"New Rush available. Bà ngoại is waiting." Light-touch notification at lunch time.
Step 02
Play 2 minutes
Rush mode. Customer queue, bowl builder, 120-second timer. Low commitment.
Step 03
Earn Đồng + streak
Score → points → Đồng currency → weekly challenge progress.
Step 04
See sponsor nudge
"Craving real phở? Phở 79, 0.8 mi." Result screen links to the restaurant page.
Step 05
Redeem or share
Coupon redemption at partner restaurant, or share score card to social.
Step 06
Return tomorrow
New dishes unlock. New customers queue. New sponsor of the week.
Key insight: The game is a wrapper around the loyalty currency. Every session deposits Đồng into the user's wallet, and Đồng is only redeemable at real restaurants. The cashier's counter is the actual "endgame."

05 · Revenue Model

How it pays for itself

Three distinct revenue streams, none of which require user-facing ads or in-app purchases. All revenue comes from the restaurant directory partnership side.

Sponsorship tiers

Listing
$0/mo
  • Appears in "nearby" nudges
  • Profile page in directory
  • Accepts Đồng redemption
  • Basic analytics
Featured Week
$199/week
  • Home banner placement
  • Result screen nudge (all players)
  • Custom weekly challenge
  • Attribution dashboard
  • ~10,000 impressions/week
Seasonal Partner
$2k/event
  • Tết / Mid-Autumn / Summer event
  • Branded dish unlock
  • Custom reward coupon
  • 2-week campaign + recap report
  • Cross-promo in email + push

Revenue math — Year 1 conservative

Featured weeks: 52 weeks × $199 × 70% sell-through = $7,244
Seasonal partners: 4 events × $2,000 × 2 partners each = $16,000
Total Year 1 ARR: ~$23,000 with no paid marketing spend

Year 2 target: open Featured Week auctions (demand likely exceeds supply in Little Saigon), add premium placement tier (home takeover for Tết week: $5k). Realistic $50–75k ARR by end of Year 2.

06 · Build Roadmap

Six-week MVP, then expansion

The goal is to ship the core loop fast, validate retention, then expand. Phase 1 is the arcade game. Phases 2–3 add the monetization and social layers.

01
Weeks 1–3
Core game (MVP)
Ship phở-only gameplay with Quán Mode + Daily Rush. Embedded as a new tab in Little Saigon Official. No monetization yet. Goal: prove the retention hook.
  • SwiftUI game view inside existing app shell
  • 12 ingredients, 8 customer variants, phở bò & phở gà orders
  • Firestore-backed Đồng wallet integration
  • Cloud Function for daily leaderboard aggregation
  • TestFlight beta with 20–30 users
02
Weeks 4–6
Sponsor layer + public launch
Add Featured Restaurant banner, weekly challenges, and coupon redemption flow. Soft-launch to existing Little Saigon user base with 3 pilot restaurant partners.
  • Admin dashboard for restaurant partners (web)
  • QR coupon redemption flow + fraud prevention
  • Attribution tracking (game session → restaurant visit)
  • Share card image generation (canvas → Photos)
  • Press kit + launch announcement via Little Saigon channels
03
Months 3–4
Content expansion
Add 5 new dishes, each with unique gameplay mechanics. Expand customer roster. Launch shop tier progression and cosmetic upgrades.
  • Bún bò Huế (spice slider), bánh xèo (timed flip), cơm tấm (plating)

07 · Success Metrics

What "working" looks like

Four metrics that matter. Every other number is noise. Review weekly in Phase 1, monthly after Phase 2.

D7 Retention
30%+
% of new users who play again within 7 days. Benchmark: casual-game median is 20–25%.
Daily Active / MAU
20%+
DAU/MAU ratio — a strong daily-habit app sits at 20%+. Wordle peaked near 30%.
Coupon Redemption
5%+
% of weekly challenge completers who actually redeem at the restaurant. This is the money metric.
Share Rate
8%+
% of Rush runs that result in a share card export. Free acquisition channel.
Kill criteria: If D7 retention sits below 15% after 8 weeks of iteration, the core loop is broken. Pivot to a simpler daily mechanic (e.g., "Tên món này là gì?" Wordle-style dish-guessing) rather than doubling down.
08 · Technical Stack

Built on what we already have

No new services. Every component of Phở Master plugs into the existing Little Saigon Official architecture. This keeps ops burden flat and lets us ship in weeks, not months.

Client
SwiftUI (iOS 17+), embedded as a tab in existing Little Saigon app. Shared MVVM patterns.
Backend
Firebase — same project (`little-saigon-c055a`). Reuses auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions.
Game state
Firestore collections: `gameSessions`, `leaderboards/daily`, `challenges/weekly`.
Đồng wallet
Existing `users/{uid}/wallet` structure. Game writes are rate-limited via Cloud Function.
Leaderboard
Cloud Function aggregation every 5 min. Cached result served from Firestore cache doc.
Sponsor CMS
Existing React admin dashboard. New "Featured Restaurant" section + scheduling UI.
Coupon redemption
Existing QR offer system in Business Owner Portal. No new infrastructure.
Analytics
Existing SessionManager.swift + Firebase Analytics. Custom events for game funnel.
Assets
SF Symbols + custom emoji-style illustrations. Food images from existing Top 50 pipeline.
Share cards
SwiftUI → UIImage rendering. Share sheet export to Photos / Messages / Instagram.
Build effort estimate: Phase 1 (core game) — ~120 engineering hours. Phase 2 (sponsor layer) — ~80 hours. Can ship MVP with existing Little Saigon dev capacity; no new hires needed.
09 · Risks & Mitigations

What could go wrong

Every plan has failure modes. These are the ones worth planning for — and the ones we've already thought about how to handle.

High
Casual games have brutal retention curves
Most casual games lose 70% of users by day 7. Mitigation: the game doesn't need to retain on its own merit — it needs to retain as part of the Little Saigon app. Daily Rush + Đồng integration + real restaurant rewards are the three cords that make retention possible.
High
Restaurants won't pay for sponsorship if the audience is small
Phase 2 sponsor launch requires an audience to sell. Mitigation: give the first 3–5 partners free featured weeks in exchange for testimonials and attribution data. Use that data to justify pricing to future partners.
Medium
Đồng inflation from aggressive gameplay rewards
If players earn Đồng faster than restaurants can redeem, the currency devalues. Mitigation: per-day earning cap (500₫), scaling rewards to difficulty, and economy simulation before setting final numbers.
Medium
Cultural missteps in gameplay or customer characters
Caricature is a real risk. Mitigation: playtest with Vietnamese-American focus group (bà ngoại demographic included), get sign-off from at least 2 community advisors, and include Vietnamese-language in-app review prompt specifically for cultural feedback.
Low
App Store rejection due to "gambling" framing
Apple sometimes flags games with real-world currency redemption. Mitigation: Đồng is framed as loyalty points (not currency), coupon values are fixed ($ off, not cash), and redemption is structured like any restaurant loyalty program.
Low
Engineering bandwidth conflict with Little Saigon roadmap
Game work competes with Events & Marketplace expansions. Mitigation: strict 6-week MVP scope; if game doesn't hit retention targets by Week 8, pause expansion and refocus on directory.
